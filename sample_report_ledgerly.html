Findings

  • Across 10,000 simulated futures, Ledgerly survives the full 36-month horizon in 65% of them.
  • When it fails, the median time of death is month 14, and 13% of failures happen in the first year.
  • The number one killer is customer acquisition cost: it is the decisive factor in 39% of failures.
  • If customer acquisition cost lands in its worst 10% ($1,299), survival falls to 29%; in its best 10% ($712) it rises to 97%.
  • To reach 80% survival, customer acquisition cost must be at most $844. Only 29% of the range you assumed clears that bar.
  • The strongest decision tested is “Delay two engineering hires”, which moves survival from 65% to 90% (+25 points).
  • Median LTV to CAC is 3.3x (a common benchmark is 3x or more) and CAC is paid back in about 8 months.
